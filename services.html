Applied analytic services

Applied analytics services.

Technical depth is scoped around a real decision. Acropolis supports agencies, research teams, care organizations, and mission-driven partners with defensible data foundations, models, surveys, and reports.

Capabilities

Support across the analytic lifecycle.

Engagements can focus on one specific deliverable or combine several capabilities into a documented workflow that can be updated over time.

01

Population & survey data integration

Create a clean, defensible data backbone before modeling begins.

  • BRFSS, YRBSS, Census/ACS, vital statistics, and surveillance data
  • Geographic and temporal harmonization
  • Variable crosswalks, recodes, and data dictionaries
  • Structures designed for repeat updates
02

Modeling, burden & risk

Develop estimates and scenarios that answer a defined planning or research question.

  • Survey-weighted and multivariable regression
  • Small-area burden and risk summaries
  • Trend, cluster, and geographic comparisons
  • Scenario analysis with uncertainty made explicit
03

Survey & program analytics

Help programs collect the right information and use it to improve services and communicate need.

  • Survey design, refinement, and analysis
  • Caregiver and patient-experience reporting
  • Recurring program summaries and dashboards
  • Links between participant experience and population context
04

Reporting, visualization & grants

Translate complex methods into materials tailored to reviewers, leaders, partners, and public audiences.

  • Publication-ready figures, maps, and tables
  • Technical reports, executive briefs, and slide decks
  • Analytic and methods sections for proposals
  • Codebooks, appendices, and reproducible handoffs
Engagement process

Start narrow. Build what the question needs.

The initial conversation is used to determine whether Acropolis is a good methodological and practical fit before a detailed scope is developed.

1

Scope the decision

Define the question, intended audience, available data, constraints, timeline, and what a useful answer would look like.

2

Design the work

Align methods, data governance, milestones, deliverables, review points, and expectations for code and documentation.

3

Deliver and hand off

Produce decision-ready outputs with the technical context needed to interpret, maintain, reproduce, or extend them.

Typical partners

Where the work fits.

  • State and local public-health agencies
  • Universities and interdisciplinary research teams
  • Clinical, memory-care, and long-term-care organizations
  • Nonprofit and mission-driven programs
  • Laboratory and translational science groups

Engagement range

Work may range from a bounded analytic consultation to a repeat reporting system or multi-year research collaboration. Scope depends on data access, governance requirements, method, audience, and timeline.

Do not send protected health information, confidential records, or restricted datasets during an initial inquiry. Secure transfer and appropriate agreements can be discussed after scope and fit are reviewed.

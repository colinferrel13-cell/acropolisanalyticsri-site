Email a project inquiry Ask about participation

What to include

  1. The research, policy, program, or grant question you are working on
  2. Your organization and the audience the work needs to support
  3. The data you have or expect to obtain
  4. Known constraints, approvals, or data-use requirements
  5. Your preferred timeline and anticipated deliverables
Before you write

Keep the first message non-confidential.

Do not include protected health information, personally identifiable data, credentials, confidential records, or restricted files in an initial email. If a project moves forward, appropriate agreements and transfer methods can be discussed before data is exchanged.

For more information about this website's current data practices, read the privacy notice. Planned public surveys and their current recruitment status are listed on the participation hub.

About the institute

Built at the intersection of epidemiology and translational science.

Acropolis Analytics Research Institute was founded by Colin Ferrel, MPH, an epidemiologist and nanomedicine researcher whose work spans infectious disease, chronic disease surveillance, caregiver health, and modular biomedical technologies.

The institute's guiding idea is simple: rigorous science should travel beyond an analysis and become evidence that improves medicine, policy, and measurable outcomes.

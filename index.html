How Acropolis works

One rigorous approach, two ways to work.

The same epidemiology-led, reproducible approach supports independent research and carefully scoped partner work.

Build the evidence base

Harmonize surveillance, survey, census, vital-statistics, and program data across definitions, places, and years.

Analyze burden & uncertainty

Develop estimates, trends, risk summaries, and scenarios while keeping bias, survey design, and uncertainty visible.

Translate evidence into use

Create maps, figures, briefs, reports, and reusable workflows for scientific and operational audiences.

Research collaboration

Develop shared methods, co-designed studies, reproducible data systems, manuscripts, and grant-ready analytic plans.

About the institute

Independent by design, collaborative in practice.

Acropolis Analytics Research Institute, LLC is a founder-led independent research and consulting organization. Founded by Colin Ferrel, MPH, it connects epidemiology, translational science, and decision-focused analytics.

Current work spans population health, caregiver research, infectious and chronic disease, and the reusable systems needed to carry evidence into practice.

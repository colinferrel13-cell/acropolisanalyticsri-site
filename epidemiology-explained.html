Series roadmap

Choose a subject, not a starting line.

These are the eight planned subject areas. The roadmap describes the scope of the series; only items in the released list above are available to read.

  1. Foundations

    How epidemiology relates to public health and clinical care.

  2. Understanding Health Numbers

    Reading counts, risks, rates, uncertainty, charts, and maps.

  3. Disease Biology and Clinical Concepts

    How disease develops and how signs, symptoms, and stages differ.

  4. Prevention, Testing and Treatment

    Distinguishing prevention, screening, diagnosis, and treatment.

  5. Evidence and Study Design

    How researchers design studies and where bias can enter.

  6. Statistical Models and Analytical Methods

    What models estimate, and what their results cannot establish.

  7. Transmission, Outbreaks and Infectious-Disease Modeling

    How researchers describe spread and investigate outbreaks.

  8. Healthcare Systems and Public Communication

    How to explain evidence, uncertainty, and trade-offs to different audiences.

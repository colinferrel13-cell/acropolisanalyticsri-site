Library structure

Three routes to the work.

Categories remain together while the collection is small and can expand into dedicated libraries as the research portfolio grows.

Data

Data & maps

Documented tables, extracts, geospatial layers, figures, crosswalks, and code lists prepared for responsible reuse.

Reporting

Reports & briefs

Technical reports, executive summaries, policy briefs, methods notes, and other decision-focused materials.

Scholarship

Publications & presentations

Peer-reviewed work, conference materials, talks, and selected prior contributions with roles and affiliations made clear.

Release standard

Context travels with every output.

Documentation is part of the work, not an optional addition.

01

Context & methods

Purpose, population, geography, period, sources, transformations, models, and quality checks.

02

Limits & privacy

Uncertainty, bias, missingness, comparability, suppression, permissions, and uses the output cannot support.

03

Citation & reuse

Version, release date, authorship, role, suggested citation, license or use conditions, and a route for questions.

Participant-derived outputs receive additional review. Reviewed aggregate findings may be posted here. Raw responses, repeat-participation records, precise timestamps, direct identifiers, and unreviewed free text will not be public releases.

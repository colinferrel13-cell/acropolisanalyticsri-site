ADRD Caregiver Experience Survey

A planned repeatable survey to better understand the experiences, responsibilities, health, resource access, and unmet needs of adults who provide unpaid care to people living with Alzheimer's disease and related dementias.

Participation status

This study is not open.

The questionnaire and participant materials are in development. There is no active form, registration, or collection of responses on this page.

Bookmark this page for eligibility, time commitment, consent, and the link to the survey if recruitment opens. For other studies, visit the survey directory.

Who the study is for

Planned study at a glance

Audience
Adults providing unpaid care to someone living with Alzheimer’s disease or a related dementia
Geography
United States
Recall period
Caregiving during the previous year
Repeat participation
Proposed: one response per eligible person in a rolling 365-day period

These are design goals, not final eligibility rules. The annual verification method has not been selected and no participation control is active now.

What we plan to ask about

  • Time and responsibilities: what the caregiver does, how often, and what changes over time.
  • Effects on the caregiver: health, emotional strain, employment, and household finances.
  • Access to support: what services are available, what is missing, and what information would help.

Questions or accessibility needs? Contact the study team. Please do not email survey answers or personal health information.

Privacy & public findings

Before recruitment opens, this page will explain consent, what the collection service processes, who can access answers, what is kept and for how long, and the limits of confidentiality. It will also explain any repeat-participation checks separately from the answers themselves.

Public reporting is intended to use reviewed, aggregate findings. Raw responses, unreviewed free text, and verification records will not be published. Any public data release should include a data dictionary, limitations, and disclosure review.

In development · not accepting responses Caregiver research

ADRD Caregiver Experience Survey.

A planned repeatable survey to better understand the experiences, responsibilities, health, resource access, and unmet needs of adults who provide unpaid care to people living with Alzheimer's disease and related dementias.

P

This study is not yet open.

No survey form is active on this page, and no responses are being recorded. The questionnaire, participant materials, privacy controls, annual participation approach, and data-release plan are being prepared before recruitment begins.

Study overview

Designed around the caregiver experience.

The planned survey focuses on unpaid caregiving within the previous year and is intended to support responsible, repeatable analysis over time.

Final eligibility, consent language, question wording, participation controls, and reporting rules will be posted before the survey opens.

Planned domains

What the project is designed to study.

The final instrument will be refined and versioned before launch so that changes can be documented across future collection periods.

Care context

Responsibilities & time

Caregiving activities, weekly time, duration, living arrangements, and changes in responsibility.

Caregiver impact

Health, work & finances

Physical and mental health, emotional and financial stress, employment changes, and coping approaches.

Support environment

Information & unmet needs

Access to services, satisfaction with resources, communication access, and the support caregivers wish they had.

Privacy & participation

Study-specific information will come first.

Before answering questions, prospective participants will be shown what participation involves, what information is processed, how repeat participation is managed, who can access responses, and the limits of confidentiality.

The verification method for the 365-day response goal has not been selected. Before launch, this page will explain what signal is used, what is retained, how long it is kept, and how it is separated from survey answers. No repeat-participation control is active now.

Results and public data

Public reporting is expected to focus on reviewed, aggregated findings. Any downloadable release will include methods, limitations, a data dictionary, and disclosure review. Verification records, raw free text, and restricted response data will not be public releases.

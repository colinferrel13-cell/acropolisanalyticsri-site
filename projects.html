Program in development Current research program

Population-Adjusted Epidemiology Outcomes Network

PAEON is a unified county-level outcomes engine that aligns population denominators, behavioral risk, morbidity, mortality, structural context, and access to care within a reusable analytic framework.

The program is designed to support fairer comparisons across counties, states, rural and urban settings, and years. Each workstream applies the same documented backbone to a different health or policy domain.

Program architecture

Unit
County-year and regional comparisons
Inputs
Population, survey, surveillance, mortality, access, and contextual data
Methods
Harmonization, small-area estimation, trend analysis, and multivariable modeling
Outputs
Documented datasets, maps, risk summaries, methods notes, and decision briefs

Survey in development · not recruiting Current survey project

ADRD caregiver experience & burden

A planned repeatable survey of adults who provide unpaid care to people living with Alzheimer's disease or a related dementia, designed to describe caregiver responsibilities, health, support, and unmet needs over time.

What the project is designed to measure

  • Caregiving time, tasks, and changes in responsibility
  • Effects on employment, finances, physical health, and mental health
  • Services used, services unavailable, and unmet needs
  • Patterns that can inform program design and resource advocacy

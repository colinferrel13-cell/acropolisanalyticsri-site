Health topics

Choose a topic to see its published resources. Some topics do not yet have any releases.

  • Bones, joints & immune conditions

    Musculoskeletal and autoimmune conditions, including arthritis, osteoporosis, spinal disorders, and lupus.

  • Brain, nerves & senses

    Dementia, neurologic and neurodevelopmental conditions, and conditions affecting hearing or vision.

  • Cancer

    Cancer by site or type, along with research on screening, prevention, outcomes, and access to care.

  • Diabetes, metabolism & kidney health

    Diabetes, obesity, cholesterol disorders, and kidney disease, including research on how these conditions overlap.

  • Digestive, liver & oral health

    Digestive conditions, liver and gallbladder disease, pancreatitis, and conditions affecting teeth and gums.

  • Heart, blood & circulation

    Cardiovascular disease and blood disorders, including coronary artery disease, stroke, high blood pressure, anemia, and sickle cell disease.

  • Infections & outbreaks

    Communicable diseases, outbreaks, and post-infectious conditions. Research may concern respiratory, foodborne, vector-borne, or sexually transmitted infections.

  • Injuries, violence & harmful exposures

    Falls, traffic injuries, violence, poisoning, and occupational or environmental exposures.

  • Lungs & breathing

    Asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), occupational lung disease, and other respiratory conditions.

  • Mental health & substance use

    Depression, anxiety, mental health conditions, alcohol and drug use disorders, and overdose.

  • Reproductive, pregnancy & early-life health

    Reproductive health, pregnancy-related conditions, preterm birth, low birth weight, and congenital conditions.

Collections

Browse public announcements or explore research on agriculture and health. Material in these collections can also appear under a health topic.

  • Agriculture

    Agricultural research and public health, including farmworker health, occupational exposures, food safety, and the relationships among people, animals, and their environment.

  • Public service announcements (PSAs)

    Public-facing announcements and educational materials about health and safety. A PSA can address any condition or stand on its own.

Reproductive Health & STIs

Conditions affecting the reproductive organs outside of pregnancy and childhood, such as endometriosis, as well as sexually transmitted infections (STIs), including chlamydia, gonorrhea, syphilis, and HIV. A condition does not have to be infectious to belong here.

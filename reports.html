Library categories

Different formats for different readers.

Each entry will identify its audience, project, year, status, author role, and reuse or distribution conditions.

Reports

Technical reports & briefs

Project reports, executive summaries, policy briefs, and methodological appendices written for partner or public audiences.

Presentations

Talks & slide decks

Conference presentations, invited talks, webinars, and annotated decks where redistribution is permitted.

Scholarship

Publications & prior work

Peer-reviewed articles and selected earlier outputs with a clear note describing Colin Ferrel's or the institute's role.

Entry standard

Context travels with the document.

A useful research library is more than a list of PDFs. Each item should make authorship, status, scope, and reuse conditions visible.

Citation & attribution

Complete title, authors, year, publisher or venue, persistent link, and a suggested citation when appropriate.

Role & affiliation

A concise description of the institute's or founder's contribution and the affiliation under which the work was completed.

Status & audience

Clear labels for peer-reviewed, final report, working paper, preprint, presentation, or other publication status.

Access & reuse

Download or external link, permissions, license or distribution limits, and a contact route for questions.

Questions & reuse

Looking for a specific report or prior contribution?

Send the title, project, topic, or intended use. Availability depends on publication status, partner agreements, and distribution permissions.

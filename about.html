About Acropolis

Acropolis Analytics Research Institute, LLC is an independent research and analytics organization. Its work connects public-health questions with evidence that researchers, communities, and program teams can use.

Mission & approach

Two ways we work

Independent research

Acropolis develops questions and methods for comparing population health across places and understanding the experience of unpaid caregivers. Public project pages distinguish plans from results.

Work with partners

Applied analytics begins with a partner’s decision or research question. The scope can include survey design, population analysis, maps, or reporting built for a specific audience.

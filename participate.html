Status guide

Know what each label means.

In development
Study design, review, or technical preparation is underway. Participation is not open.
Open
The study is accepting eligible participants through the linked survey.
Paused or closed
No new responses are being accepted. The project page remains available for context.
Results available
Reviewed findings, methods, or approved public data have been released.
What to expect

Participation begins with context.

Every active project will explain what participation involves before a visitor reaches the questionnaire.

01

Review the study

Read the purpose, eligibility, estimated time, topics, contacts, and current project status.

02

Make an informed choice

Review the study-specific consent and privacy information before deciding whether to participate.

03

Follow the work

Return to the project page for updates, reviewed findings, reports, and approved public data when available.

Participant information

Clear expectations for every study.

Participation will be voluntary. Active studies will identify what data are requested, how technical controls work, who can access responses, how long information is retained, and whom to contact with questions.

Responsible public reporting

Public results will be released only after review for privacy, disclosure risk, sampling limitations, and appropriate interpretation. Raw response systems and participant-verification information will not be presented as public downloads.

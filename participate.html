Status guide

Know what each label means.

In development
Study design, review, or technical preparation is underway. Participation is not open.
Open for participation
The study is accepting eligible participants through the linked survey.
Paused
Recruitment is temporarily stopped. The project page remains available for updates.
Closed
No new responses are being accepted for the completed collection period.

“Outputs available” may appear separately when reviewed materials are posted; it does not mean recruitment is open.

What to expect

Participation begins with context.

Every active project will explain what participation involves before a visitor reaches the questionnaire.

01

Review the study

Read the purpose, eligibility, estimated time, topics, contacts, and current project status.

02

Make an informed choice

Review what data are requested, how repeat participation is managed, who can access responses, how long information is retained, and the limits of confidentiality before deciding whether to participate.

03

Follow the work

Return to the study page for recruitment updates and to Research Outputs for reviewed findings and approved public materials.

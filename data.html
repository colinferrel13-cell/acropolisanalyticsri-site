Library structure

What will appear here.

Releases will be grouped by project and clearly labeled so a reader can understand what a file represents without having participated in the original analysis.

Data

Tables & extracts

Aggregated, machine-readable files such as county summaries, indicator panels, and approved regional extracts.

Geospatial

Layers & maps

Reusable geographic layers, modeled surfaces, and export-ready graphics with projection and source details.

Reference

Crosswalks & code lists

Stable variable mappings, geographic crosswalks, value definitions, and other materials that support downstream use.

Release standard

Every public release should answer four questions.

Documentation is treated as part of the dataset, not an optional addition.

01

What is this?

Project, topic, population, geography, period, unit of observation, variables, and intended uses.

02

How was it created?

Sources, inclusion rules, transformations, models, suppression decisions, and quality checks.

03

What are the limits?

Uncertainty, bias, missingness, comparability constraints, and uses the release cannot support.

04

How should it be cited?

Version, release date, persistent location, attribution, license or use conditions, and contact information.

Participant research

Survey releases cross a separate review boundary.

Public survey outputs will normally begin with reviewed aggregate findings. Raw responses, repeat-participation verification records, volunteer contact information, direct identifiers, precise timestamps, and unreviewed free text will not be posted in this library.

Context travels with the release

Any participant-derived release will identify the instrument version, field dates, eligibility and sampling limitations, transformations, aggregation or suppression rules, and appropriate uses.

Data governance

Public does not mean unrestricted.

Public releases will focus on aggregated or otherwise approved outputs. Individual-level, partner-confidential, protected, or contract-restricted data will not be posted in this library.

Custom analysis

A public release may support a new question without containing the exact geography, time period, or model a partner needs. Custom extracts and analyses can be scoped separately when data permissions allow.
